Frequently asked

What is KRYSTAL?

KRYSTAL is an app that brings your robot to life. KRYSTAL gives it a personality (that you determine), so it can hold dynamic conversations and express itself through its sounds and movements. Its reactions feel like expressions of who it is, not pre-programmed instructions or actions. Everything runs privately on your device, and it works at home, on the road, or anywhere else you take it.

Do I need a physical unit to use it?

No. KRYSTAL works on its own in Mood Responses mode, and can be set up without a unit. With a compatible unit, KRYSTAL connects over Bluetooth to bring it to life.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your voice is processed entirely on your device and is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy below for full details.

Why does KRYSTAL ask for microphone and Bluetooth access?

The microphone lets KRYSTAL transcribe your speech on-device so it can react to the words you use — not the sound of your voice. Bluetooth is used only to connect to your unit. Listening is user-enabled and may continue while KRYSTAL is in the background so it can respond with audible chimes. Force-quitting the app ends the active session.
